Why Grisaille

Fewer decisions, made earlier. Everything else gets easier.

A grisaille is a painting made only in greys, to look like carved stone. We borrowed the method. Proportion, weight and rhythm get settled before any colour can hide a weak decision.

  • Form first

    Every project starts in grey. If it holds without colour, it will hold with it.

  • A small team

    You talk to the three people who do the work.

  • Fixed scope

    A price and a date before we begin. Changes are discussed, not invoiced by surprise.

  • Made to keep

    You receive the files, the fonts and a guide you can use without us.

Method

Grisaille

Four steps, in this order. Colour is third.

Each step ends with something you can look at and approve.

  1. 01

    Study

    We visit, read and ask. You tell us what the work is for.

  2. 02

    Form

    Layout, proportion and type, in grey only.

  3. 03

    Tone

    Colour enters, one decision at a time.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Files, guidelines and launch, with a call to walk you through them.

Approach

Grisaille (n.) — a painting in grey that imitates relief.

Colour is the last decision, not the first. We would rather show you a grey that works than a palette that distracts.

Sculptors have always tested a figure in plaster before touching marble. Our drafts are the plaster.
