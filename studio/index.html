Studio.

About

Grisaille began in 2019, in a rented room above a print shop in Lisbon. We kept the name of the old technique: a painting in grey, made to look like stone.

A fictional studio for a design concept.

Story

Inês Valadares started the studio after ten years in publishing and cultural institutions, where she learned that most design problems are structure problems. Tomás Brandão joined to build the websites. Mara Quintela came from printing, and taught us what paper does to an idea.

We still work from one table. We take few projects a year, and we say no when the brief needs a bigger team than ours.

Team

Three people. All three on every project.

Ten years of cultural publishing, one of web, one of print. Each stayed because the work is small enough to hold in one head.

Principles

Rules.

  1. 01

    Form before colour

    If it only works in colour, it does not work.

  2. 02

    Say less

    One idea per page. One sentence per idea.

  3. 03

    Make it last

    Choices should survive five years and a new owner.

  4. 04

    Stay close

    No hand-offs. The person you brief is the person who designs.

We are not the right studio for everyone. If your brief starts with a colour, we may ask to start with a grey.
